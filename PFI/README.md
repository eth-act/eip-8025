# EIP-8025 proposal for inclusion

EIP-8025 was proposed for inclusion in Hegota during **All Core Devs -
Consensus (ACDC) #178 on May 14, 2026**. Francesco Risitano and Ignacio
Hagopian presented the proposal on behalf of the EF zkEVM team.

The presentation starts at approximately **01:08:45 in the call recording**.
Forkcast records the proposal and presentation at that timestamp; its corrected
transcript begins the segment at `01:12:44.680` because the transcript and video
use different time origins.

## Presentation artifacts

- [`slides.md`](slides.md) is the original nine-slide Marp source.
- [`assets/proof-flow.png`](assets/proof-flow.png) is the diagram shown on slide
  2.
- [`source-diagrams/proof-flow.excalidraw`](source-diagrams/proof-flow.excalidraw)
  is the editable source for that diagram.
- `tldr-diagram.png` and its Excalidraw source are retained because they were in
  the upstream deck directory at presentation time. The final pre-call edit
  replaced this older diagram with `proof-flow.png`, so it is not referenced by
  `slides.md`.

The artifacts are copied from `frisitano/slides` at commit
[`324a88e3e2816aeecaf8a09b2e8d1439bdf4796f`](https://github.com/frisitano/slides/tree/324a88e3e2816aeecaf8a09b2e8d1439bdf4796f/presentations/eip8025-acdc-proposal-2026-05-14),
the final commit on `main` before the call began. Later changes to the live deck
are intentionally not reflected here.
